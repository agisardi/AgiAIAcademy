Who We Are

Founders Agi & Zac Sardi

We are educators and innovators passionate about making artificial intelligence approachable, engaging, and useful for everyone. Our program brings together expertise in teaching, technology, and lifelong learning to create a hands-on path into the world of AI.

🎯 Our Mission

To demystify AI through practice, not theory. We believe AI should be a tool anyone can explore, understand, and use — not just computer scientists and engineers.

🛠️ What We Do

  • Understand core AI concepts without jargon
  • Experiment with real-world AI tools (chatbots, image generation, data analysis)
  • Build simple projects that spark creativity and confidence

🔮 Our Vision

We see a future where AI literacy is as common as computer literacy, empowering people of all ages to engage meaningfully with technology and shape its role in society.

🌍 Who We Serve

  • 👩‍💼 Mid-career professionals seeking to upskill
  • 👵 Retirees and lifelong learners
  • 👩‍🏫 Educators, community leaders, and hobbyists
